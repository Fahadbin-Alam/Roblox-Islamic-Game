# Seerah campaign checklist

The campaign has 30 chapters in order, from the Year of the Elephant to the passing of the
Prophet ﷺ. Chapters 1 to 17 take place in historical Makkah and 18 to 30 in historical Madinah.
Each chapter opens only after the one before it. A chapter is: travel to the place, read the
story (people, events), do the activity if there is one, then answer the decision question,
which explains what really happened and its consequence.

The data lives in `src/ReplicatedStorage/Shared/Data/SeerahCampaign.lua`. Players see the same
checklist in game under **Journeys > Seerah**, with a tick for every finished chapter.

Every chapter also carries a *GameplayNote* that says which parts are documented and which are
game inventions (for example "carrying water skins is a game task").

Review status: written from the listed sources, **not yet reviewed by a scholar**. See
[sources.md](sources.md).

opens. The puzzles are game inventions and each room says so (*FictionNote*).
